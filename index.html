Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting bicycles, guided tours, safety, and payments.

How do I rent and unlock a bicycle?

Renting is completely contactless and takes less than 60 seconds! Simply open the PedalCity website or web app, pick your nearest station, select your bicycle model, and scan the QR code on the handlebar lock to release the bike.

Do I need to pay a security deposit?

No security deposit is required for standard city and mountain bikes when you verify with a valid government ID or mobile OTP. Premium e-bikes require a nominal pre-authorization hold that is released immediately upon return.

What safety equipment is included?

Every bicycle rental includes a sanitized lightweight safety helmet, high-decibel bell, front and rear LED safety lights, a waterproof phone mount, and a heavy-duty combination cable lock at zero extra charge.

Can I return the bike to a different station?

Yes! You can pick up a bike from any of our 15+ city hubs and return it to any other PedalCity docking station across the city without any additional one-way or drop-off fees.

What if I experience a flat tire or breakdown?

Our 24/7 Mobile Support Team is on standby. In the rare event of a puncture or mechanical issue, tap "Request Assistance" on your dashboard or call our helpline, and our mobile van will repair or swap your bicycle within 15–20 minutes.
