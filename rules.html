Rental Rules

Rental Guidelines

Please read and follow these rules for a safe and enjoyable experience.

Age & Eligibility Requirements

Riders must be at least 16 years old to rent a standard bicycle and 18+ for e-bikes. Riders under 18 require a parent or guardian's written consent. A valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Driving License) is required at the time of pickup.

ID Proof & Verification

All renters must present a valid photo ID at pickup. We accept Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID. International visitors may use their passport. A digital copy stored on your phone is also acceptable.

Riding Safety Rules

Helmets must be worn at all times. Follow all traffic rules and signals. Use dedicated bike lanes wherever available. Do not ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Children under 12 must use bikes with training wheels and be accompanied by an adult.

Damage & Loss Policy

Minor wear and tear is expected. Major damage (bent frame, punctured tires, broken components) will be assessed by our team. Repair costs will be deducted from the security deposit. In case of theft or total loss, the full replacement cost applies. Report all damage immediately upon return.

Late Return Fees

A grace period of 30 minutes is allowed. After that, late fees apply at ₹50/hour for city bikes, ₹75/hour for mountain bikes, and ₹100/hour for e-bikes. If the bike is not returned within 24 hours of the due time without notice, it will be reported as lost.

Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is required at the time of rental. The deposit is fully refunded upon safe return of the bicycle.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Can I return the bike to a different station?

Yes! You can return your bike to any PedalCity station. Multi-station returns are included at no extra cost with Half-Day and Full-Day plans.

What happens if it rains during my rental?

If heavy rain is forecasted, we offer free rescheduling up to 2 hours before your booking. For ongoing rain, you may extend your rental for free up to 1 hour.

Do you provide helmets and safety gear?

A basic helmet is included with every rental at no additional cost. Premium helmets with visors and padding are available as add-ons for ₹49/day.

Can I extend my rental duration?

Absolutely! You can extend your rental through the PedalCity dashboard or by calling our support line. Extensions are charged at the hourly rate for your bike type.

Is there a cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 4 hours before your booking. Cancellations within 4 hours incur a 25% fee. No-shows are charged 50% of the booking amount.

Do you offer corporate or event bookings?

Yes! We offer custom packages for corporate team outings, events, and celebrations. Contact us for a tailored quote with group discounts of up to 30%.
